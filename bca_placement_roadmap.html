// PRO TIPS
Golden Rules for No-Placement College Students
01
Self-placement IS possible. Most top engineers at Google and Amazon from India got in off-campus. Your college's placement cell does not define your ceiling — your daily effort does.
02
LinkedIn is your placement cell. Optimize your profile with a headline like "BCA Student | C++ | DSA | React | Open to SDE roles". Connect with 500+ people. Message recruiters directly — it works more than you think.
03
Consistency beats intensity. Solving 3 problems a day for 2 years (2,190 problems) is infinitely better than grinding 30 problems for one week then burning out. Show up every single day.
04
Don't skip internships. Even a 2-month unpaid internship at a startup makes your resume 10x better. It shows real-world experience and companies ask about it in every interview.
05
BCA degree is not a barrier. Companies like Zoho, Amazon, and many startups explicitly hire BCA graduates. Skills matter more than degree tier — prove it with your GitHub, LeetCode rating, and live projects.
06
Participate in online contests. Unstop, HackerEarth, CodeChef, and company-specific hackathons (Amazon Hackathon, Flipkart Grid, etc.) directly lead to interviews and PPOs. These are golden shortcuts.
07
Track everything in a spreadsheet. Track companies applied, OA given, interviews cleared, offers received. Job hunting is a numbers game — apply to 100+ places. A 5% conversion is great and gives you 5 offers.
08
Build in public. Post your projects, learning journey, and LeetCode milestones on LinkedIn. Recruiters follow and reach out. Multiple students have gotten cold interview calls just from posting "I solved 100 LeetCode problems today."
You don't need a fancy college.
