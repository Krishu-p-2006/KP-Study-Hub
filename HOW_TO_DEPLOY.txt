HOW TO DEPLOY (GitHub Pages — FREE, EASY, FOREVER)
====================================================

STEP 1 — Create GitHub account
-------------------------------
1. Go to: https://github.com
2. Click "Sign up" → use your email → create account
3. Verify your email

STEP 2 — Create a new repository
----------------------------------
1. Click the green "New" button (top left)
2. Repository name: bca-study-hub
3. Set to: Public ✅
4. Click "Create repository"

STEP 3 — Upload your files
----------------------------
1. On the next page, click "uploading an existing file"
2. Drag & drop ALL files from the public/ folder:
   - index.html
   - c-study-notes.html
   - digital_electronics_study_guide__1_.html
   - organizational_behaviour__5_.html
3. Click "Commit changes" (green button)

STEP 4 — Enable GitHub Pages
------------------------------
1. Click "Settings" (top menu of your repo)
2. Scroll down to "Pages" (left sidebar)
3. Under "Source" → select "Deploy from a branch"
4. Branch: main | Folder: / (root)
5. Click Save

STEP 5 — Your site is LIVE!
-----------------------------
After about 1 minute, visit:
  https://YOUR-GITHUB-USERNAME.github.io/bca-study-hub/

Example: if your username is "krishuprajapati":
  https://krishuprajapati.github.io/bca-study-hub/

DONE! Share that link with your whole class. 🎉

================================================
HOW TO ADD NEW STUDY MATERIAL LATER
================================================

1. Go to your GitHub repo
2. Click "Add file" → "Upload files"
3. Upload the new .html file (e.g. math-notes.html)
4. Open index.html in GitHub → click the pencil ✏️ icon
5. Copy one of the existing <a class="card"> blocks
6. Change the href, title, description to match your new file
7. Click "Commit changes"

That's it! Your new subject appears on the site within minutes.
